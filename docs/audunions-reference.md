# Audition Unions Reference

A directory of every performers' union currently available in TAO's
audition project dropdown, grouped by country. Each entry shows the
union's official acronym/name and a one-line description of who it
covers.

> **Note on "Non-Union".** Every country listed below also offers a
> **Non-Union** option in the dropdown, used when a project is not
> affiliated with any performers' union. To keep this reference
> readable, that entry is described once here rather than under every
> country.
>
> **Non-Union** — Not affiliated with a performers' union; covers
> non-union productions and engagements.

---

## Australia

- **MEAA** — Media, Entertainment & Arts Alliance — Australia's union
  for performers, crew, and media workers across stage, screen, and
  journalism.

## Brazil

- **SATED-SP** — Sindicato dos Artistas e Técnicos em Espetáculos de
  Diversões — São Paulo state union for performers and technicians in
  entertainment.

## Canada

- **ACTRA** — Alliance of Canadian Cinema, Television and Radio
  Artists — English-speaking Canadian performers in film, TV, radio,
  voice, and digital media.
- **Canadian Actor's Equity** — Canadian Actors' Equity Association —
  professional artists in English-language live performance: theatre,
  opera, and dance.
- **UBCP/ACTRA** — Union of BC Performers — ACTRA's British Columbia
  branch, representing English-speaking performers in recorded media in
  BC.
- **UDA - Union des Artistes** — Quebec's union representing
  French-speaking professional performers in film, TV, radio, theatre,
  and recorded media.

## France

- **SFA-CGT** — Syndicat Français des Artistes-interprètes (CGT) —
  French performers' union for actors, dancers, singers, and circus
  artists.
- **SNAM-CGT** — Syndicat National des Artistes Musiciens (CGT) —
  French national union for professional musicians across classical,
  jazz, and contemporary music.

## Germany

- **BFFS** — Bundesverband Schauspiel — German federal association of
  screen actors for film, TV, voice, and digital media.
- **GDBA** — Genossenschaft Deutscher Bühnen-Angehöriger — German
  trade union for stage performers in theatre, opera, dance, and
  concert.

## India

- **CINTAA** — Cine and TV Artists Association — Mumbai-based Indian
  union for film and television actors.
- **FWICE** — Federation of Western India Cine Employees — umbrella
  federation of craft unions covering Bollywood performers and crew.

## Ireland

- **Irish Equity (SIPTU)** — Irish trade union for performers (a
  branch of SIPTU) covering actors, singers, dancers, and writers
  across stage and screen.

## Italy

- **SAI Slc-CGIL** — Sindacato Attori Italiani (CGIL-affiliated) —
  Italian actors' union covering performers in film, TV, theatre, and
  dubbing.

## Mexico

- **ANDA** — Asociación Nacional de Actores — Mexico's national
  actors' union for professional performers in film, TV, theatre, and
  radio.

## Netherlands

- **Kunstenbond** — Dutch trade union for performers and creative
  workers across film, TV, theatre, music, and visual arts.
- **NTB** — Nederlandse Toonkunstenaarsbond — Dutch musicians' and
  dancers' union for performers in classical music, opera, ballet, and
  concert.

## New Zealand

- **Equity NZ** — New Zealand Equity (part of MEAA) — union for
  professional performers across screen, stage, and live performance.

## South Africa

- **SAGA** — South African Guild of Actors — represents professional
  actors in film, TV, theatre, and voice across South Africa.

## Spain

- **Union de Actores y Actrices** — Spanish actors' union representing
  professional performers in film, TV, theatre, and voiceover/dubbing.

## Switzerland

- **SBKV** — Schweizerischer Bühnenkünstlerverband — Swiss
  German-speaking stage artists association for theatre, opera, and
  dance.
- **SSFV** — Schweizer Syndikat Film und Video — Swiss film and
  audiovisual union representing performers and crew in screen
  production.
- **SSRS** — Syndicat Suisse Romand du Spectacle — French-speaking
  Swiss performing arts union covering theatre and screen.

## Turkey

- **Oyuncular Sendikasi** — Turkey's actors' union representing
  professional performers in film, TV, theatre, and dubbing.

## United Kingdom

- **Equity** — UK trade union for performers and creative practitioners
  across stage, screen, and live performance, founded 1930.

## United States

- **Actor's Equity Association** — US labor union representing
  professional actors and stage managers working in live theatre,
  founded 1913.
- **AGMA** — American Guild of Musical Artists — represents soloists,
  choristers, dancers, and stage managers in US opera, ballet, and
  concert.
- **AGVA** — American Guild of Variety Artists — US union for
  performers in live variety entertainment: cabaret, circus, theme
  parks, and comedy.
- **SAG-AFTRA** — Screen Actors Guild – American Federation of
  Television and Radio Artists — US union for film, TV, radio,
  voiceover, and broadcast performers.
