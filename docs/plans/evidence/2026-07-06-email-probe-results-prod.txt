FINDING (priority = LOW, with hard guardrails if pursued)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "Same email + different name" in TAO real data is DOMINANTLY shared agency/company
   switchboard inboxes (info@/contact@/queries@/feedback@/mailbox@ + company gmails), i.e.
   DIFFERENT real people, NOT duplicates. n_names ~= n_contacts confirms distinct people.
2. The email dimension's UNIQUE contribution (different-name/same-email) is therefore mostly
   noise on this corpus. The genuine dupe pattern is n_names < n_contacts (a name repeats) —
   already the NAME dimension's job (R-4 / R-5A).
3. Guardrails REQUIRED if the follow-up is built at all:
   a. Generic-mailbox denylist (info, contact, queries, feedback, mailbox, office, hello, …)
      + non-email junk guard ("linked in").
   b. Group-size cap = 2 (email on exactly two contacts is the only plausible dupe).
   c. "Personal domain only" does NOT rescue it (production-company gmails appear at 50/21).
4. userid 11 = separate data-hygiene review (agency switchboard emails on hundreds of contacts),
   independent of dedup.

Verdict: email-dimension follow-up WO DEPRIORITIZED. Recommend name-dimension (R-5A) remains the
primary dedup investment. Decision reserved to Kevin.
