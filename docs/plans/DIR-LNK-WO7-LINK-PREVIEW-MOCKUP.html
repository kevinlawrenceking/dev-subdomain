Design rules baked in. Company / email / phone are confirm-or-cancel — the Book owns them, and a wrong value is a correction, not a local override (that keeps one meaning of “linked” instead of inventing a half-managed state). Name, photo and office are genuine user choices: filing habits and headshots are preference, not data integrity, and the office choice decides which address and phone you inherit. The phone row is the case worth arguing about at the meeting — today’s rule blanks a managed field when the Book is empty, and the modal is where that stops being invisible. Same layout serves relink (“moving from Wilhelmina to CAA — here’s what changes”).
