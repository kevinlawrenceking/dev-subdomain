The new Activity Analytics dashboard is live. It gives us a system-wide (all-users) view of the activity happening in The Actor's Office, with headline totals and month-by-month trends. I'd love for you to take a look and gut-check the numbers against what you'd expect.

Open Activity Analytics
https://app.theactorsoffice.com/app/admin-analytics/  -  log in as an admin (also in the left sidebar).

What it shows - four numbers, four trends

  • Auditions logged - how many audition roles were logged.
  • Bookings logged - how many of those actually booked (including "direct bookings," where someone booked without auditioning). Bookings are always a subset of auditions.
  • Relationships added - new contacts added to the system (network growth).
  • Reminders completed - reminders/tasks that were actually marked done.

Each number has a small trend chart beneath it so you can see the direction over time, not just the headline.

How to use it

  1. Pick a date range (top right): Last 30 days, 90 days, 12 months, or All time. Everything updates instantly.
  2. Read the four tiles for the totals in that range.
  3. Check the trend charts below each tile.
  4. "How these numbers work" button opens a plain-language explanation of exactly how each number is counted.
  5. "Export CSV" downloads the report (totals + monthly breakdown) to open in Excel.

A few things to keep in mind when checking the numbers

  • Totals run through today; charts show complete months only. We intentionally leave the current partial month off the trend lines so it doesn't look like activity is dropping at month-end. So a tile total will usually be a little higher than the last point on its chart - the difference is this month so far. That's expected.
  • "All time" starts October 2021, when we went into real production. Earlier data was testing and is excluded.
  • It's read-only and system-wide - every user's activity combined, no per-actor breakdown (a possible future addition).

If any number looks higher or lower than you'd expect, jot down the metric and date range and I'll dig into the underlying records with you.
